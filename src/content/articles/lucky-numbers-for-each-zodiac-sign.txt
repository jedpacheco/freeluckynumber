Ask an astrology fan for their lucky number and there's a decent chance they'll give you one tied to their sun sign. Lists of lucky numbers by zodiac sign show up in magazines, apps and horoscope columns everywhere. What most of those lists don't tell you is that they don't agree with each other, and there's no single official source behind them.

So this article does two things. It gives you the numbers most commonly linked to each sign, along with the reasons astrologers usually give. And it explains where these associations come from, so you can enjoy them for what they are: a tradition and a bit of fun, not a forecast.

## Where zodiac lucky numbers come from

Western astrology divides the year into twelve signs, each linked to a constellation, an element (fire, earth, air or water) and a ruling planet. Lucky numbers usually get assigned in one of three ways.

The first is the sign's position in the zodiac. Aries is the first sign, so 1 is often listed for Aries. Taurus is second, so it gets 2, and so on.

The second is the ruling planet. In older numerology systems, each of the classical planets was paired with a number. The Sun was often linked with 1, the Moon with 2, Jupiter with 3, Mercury with 5, Venus with 6, Saturn with 8 and Mars with 9. Different authors shuffle these, which is one reason lists vary.

The third is just habit. Once a number gets printed in enough horoscope columns, it sticks.

## Lucky numbers for each sign

The numbers below are the ones we see repeated most often across popular astrology sources. You'll find others in other places, and none of them is more "correct" than the rest.

### Aries (March 21 to April 19)

Commonly listed: 1, 9 and 19. The 1 comes from Aries being the first sign, and the 9 from its ruling planet, Mars. Aries is described as bold and quick to act, so a "first place" number fits the image.

### Taurus (April 20 to May 20)

Commonly listed: 2, 6 and 24. Taurus is ruled by Venus, which older systems connect with 6. The sign is linked with comfort, patience and good food.

### Gemini (May 21 to June 20)

Commonly listed: 3, 5 and 12. Gemini is the third sign and is ruled by Mercury, often paired with 5. The twins of Gemini also make people think of doubles, so you'll sometimes see 11 or 22 as well.

### Cancer (June 21 to July 22)

Commonly listed: 2, 7 and 20. Cancer is ruled by the Moon, which numerology links with 2. Cancer is the classic homebody sign, associated with family and memory.

### Leo (July 23 to August 22)

Commonly listed: 1, 4 and 10. Leo is ruled by the Sun, traditionally number 1. A confident, center-of-attention sign getting the number one seems about right.

### Virgo (August 23 to September 22)

Commonly listed: 5, 14 and 23. Like Gemini, Virgo is ruled by Mercury, so 5 shows up again. Virgo is associated with detail and order.

### Libra (September 23 to October 22)

Commonly listed: 6, 15 and 24. Libra shares Venus with Taurus, which explains the 6. The sign is linked with balance and fairness, which is fitting since 6 is an even, steady number.

### Scorpio (October 23 to November 21)

Commonly listed: 8, 11 and 18. Scorpio's traditional ruler is Mars and its modern ruler is Pluto. Popular lists often give it 8, which numerology links to power and intensity.

### Sagittarius (November 22 to December 21)

Commonly listed: 3, 9 and 12. Sagittarius is ruled by Jupiter, traditionally paired with 3. It's the traveler and optimist of the zodiac.

### Capricorn (December 22 to January 19)

Commonly listed: 4, 8 and 22. Capricorn is ruled by Saturn, which many numerologists connect with 8. Hard work and long-term goals are the Capricorn stereotype.

### Aquarius (January 20 to February 18)

Commonly listed: 4, 7 and 11. Aquarius is linked with Saturn in old astrology and Uranus in modern astrology. It's the sign of independent thinkers, and 11, the "visionary" master number, gets pinned on it often.

### Pisces (February 19 to March 20)

Commonly listed: 3, 7 and 12. Pisces is the twelfth sign and is ruled by Jupiter in traditional astrology, so 3 and 12 both show up. It's associated with imagination and empathy.

## Why the lists disagree

If you search your own sign on three different websites, you might get three different sets of numbers. That's because there isn't a central authority in astrology. Some writers go by position in the zodiac, others by planets, others by the meaning of a number in numerology. Some combine all three. And some, honestly, just pick numbers that sound nice.

It's also worth remembering that the dates for each sign shift a little from year to year, and the constellations themselves have moved relative to the calendar over the last two thousand years because of a slow wobble in the Earth's axis. Astrologers who use the tropical zodiac, which is most Western astrologers, tie the signs to the seasons rather than the stars, so they aren't bothered by this. But it does show how much of astrology is a system people agreed on, rather than something measured.

## Do zodiac lucky numbers actually work?

There's no evidence that your sun sign affects which numbers bring you good results. Large studies of astrology, including a well-known 1985 experiment published in the journal Nature by physicist Shawn Carlson, found that astrologers couldn't match people's birth charts to their personality profiles better than chance.

That said, a lucky number doesn't have to "work" to be worth having. Lots of people like having a number that feels like theirs. It can be the number you wear on a jersey, the time you set your alarm or the table number you hope to get at a wedding. As long as it's for fun, it's harmless.

The one place we'd draw a line is gambling. Picking lottery numbers based on your zodiac sign won't change your odds at all, since every combination has the same chance of being drawn.

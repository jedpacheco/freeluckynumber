A new year is a good excuse for a fresh start, and for a lot of people that includes a new lucky color and number. This guide gives you a lucky color and a pair of lucky numbers for each zodiac sign for 2027, along with a little of the thinking behind each pick.

Before we start, a clear note. These are our own playful picks, built from traditional astrology associations like each sign's element and ruling planet, plus a bit of numerology. They're not predictions and they won't change what happens to you. Think of them as a fun theme for your year, the way some people choose a word of the year.

## What numerology says about 2027

In numerology, every calendar year has a "universal year number." You get it by adding the digits of the year. For 2027, that's 2 + 0 + 2 + 7 = 11.

That's a fun one, because 11 is a master number, which numerologists usually leave unreduced. People who follow numerology describe 11 as a number of intuition, inspiration and new ideas, with a reputation for being a bit intense. If you reduce it, you get 2, which is linked with cooperation and partnership. So numerology fans tend to describe 2027 as a year about trusting your instincts and working well with others. You can read more in [master numbers 11, 22 and 33](/articles/master-numbers-11-22-33).

## What the Chinese zodiac says

In the Chinese calendar, 2027 is the Year of the Goat, sometimes called the Sheep or Ram. It starts with Lunar New Year on February 6, 2027. The Goat is traditionally described as gentle, creative and peaceful, and it's paired with the element of fire in 2027. Traditional lucky numbers for the Goat are often listed as 2 and 7, which fits neatly with 2027 itself.

## Lucky colors and numbers for each sign

### Aries (March 21 to April 19)

Lucky color: Sunset Orange. Lucky numbers: 1 and 11.

Aries is a fire sign ruled by Mars, so warm colors are a classic match. We paired Aries's traditional number 1 with the year's 11, for a sign that likes to go first.

### Taurus (April 20 to May 20)

Lucky color: Emerald Green. Lucky numbers: 6 and 20.

Taurus is an earth sign ruled by Venus, and green has long been linked with both. The 6 comes from Venus in old numerology, and 20 reduces to 2, the number of the year.

### Gemini (May 21 to June 20)

Lucky color: Golden Yellow. Lucky numbers: 5 and 14.

Gemini is an air sign ruled by Mercury, often paired with bright yellow. Both numbers reduce to 5, Mercury's number, which suits a curious, chatty sign.

### Cancer (June 21 to July 22)

Lucky color: Silver. Lucky numbers: 2 and 7.

Cancer is ruled by the Moon, and silver is the Moon's traditional color. We gave Cancer the Goat's lucky numbers, 2 and 7, since both signs are described as home-loving and caring.

### Leo (July 23 to August 22)

Lucky color: Golden Yellow. Lucky numbers: 1 and 19.

Leo is ruled by the Sun, the gold standard of astrology colors. Both 1 and 19, which reduces to 1, point back to the Sun's traditional number.

### Virgo (August 23 to September 22)

Lucky color: Midnight Navy. Lucky numbers: 5 and 23.

Virgo is an earth sign ruled by Mercury and is known for being careful and organized. Navy is calm and practical. The 23 reduces to 5, Mercury's number again.

### Libra (September 23 to October 22)

Lucky color: Rose Pink. Lucky numbers: 6 and 15.

Libra is an air sign ruled by Venus, which is linked with pink and soft colors. 15 reduces to 6, matching Venus.

### Scorpio (October 23 to November 21)

Lucky color: Cherry Red. Lucky numbers: 8 and 9.

Scorpio is a water sign with a deep, intense reputation. Red nods to its traditional ruler, Mars, and 9 is Mars's number. The 8 comes from popular astrology lists that pair Scorpio with power.

### Sagittarius (November 22 to December 21)

Lucky color: Royal Purple. Lucky numbers: 3 and 12.

Sagittarius is a fire sign ruled by Jupiter, and purple is Jupiter's traditional color. Both numbers point to 3, Jupiter's number.

### Capricorn (December 22 to January 19)

Lucky color: Midnight Navy. Lucky numbers: 4 and 8.

Capricorn is an earth sign ruled by Saturn, linked with dark, steady colors. 8 is often listed as Saturn's number, and 4 is the numerology number of the builder.

### Aquarius (January 20 to February 18)

Lucky color: Ocean Blue. Lucky numbers: 11 and 22.

Aquarius is an air sign with a reputation for big ideas, and blue is its classic color. With 2027 being an 11 year, we gave Aquarius both master numbers.

### Pisces (February 19 to March 20)

Lucky color: Ocean Blue. Lucky numbers: 3 and 7.

Pisces is a water sign ruled by Jupiter in traditional astrology, so 3 fits. 7, the number of the dreamer and thinker in numerology, suits a sign known for imagination.

## How people use lucky colors and numbers

If you like the idea, there are lots of small ways to bring a lucky color or number into your year:

- Wear your lucky color on days that matter, like an interview or a first date.
- Use your color in a journal, planner or phone wallpaper.
- Set reminders or alarms for a time with your lucky number in it.
- Pick a lucky number for small, fun choices, like which table to sit at.

The value here is mostly in the ritual. Wearing a color you like can make you feel more confident, and that's a real effect even if the color itself isn't magic.

## One honest reminder

Lucky numbers from astrology won't help you win the lottery. Every combination in a fair draw has the same odds, whatever your sign. If you're curious why, read [lottery myths about hot and cold numbers](/articles/lottery-myths-hot-and-cold-numbers).

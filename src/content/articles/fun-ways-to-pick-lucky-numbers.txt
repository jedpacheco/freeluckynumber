Everybody seems to have their own way of picking a lucky number. Some people have used the same one since childhood. Others pick a new one for every occasion. If you're looking for ideas, here are some fun ways people choose theirs, along with a quick reminder of what lucky numbers can and can't do.

## Use a date that matters

Birthdays are the most popular source. You can use the day you were born, or add up all the digits of your birth date and reduce them to one number, the way numerology does. Our [Birthday Lucky Number Calculator](/tools/birthday-lucky-number) does that math for you, and [how to find your lucky number from your birthday](/articles/how-to-find-your-lucky-number-from-your-birthday) explains five different methods. Anniversaries, a pet's adoption day or the date you moved into your home work just as well.

## Turn a name into a number

In name numerology, each letter has a value, and you add them up. It's a fun way to find a number for yourself, a partner or even a pet. Try the [Lucky Name Calculator](/tools/lucky-name-calculator), or read [name numerology: how letters turn into numbers](/articles/name-numerology-letters-to-numbers) to do it by hand.

## Borrow from tradition

Lots of cultures have numbers with special meaning. Seven is a favorite across much of the world, 8 is lucky in China and 3 shows up in countless stories. Our article on [lucky and unlucky numbers around the world](/articles/lucky-and-unlucky-numbers-around-the-world) has plenty of ideas. You could also look up the numbers traditionally linked with [your zodiac sign](/articles/lucky-numbers-for-each-zodiac-sign).

## Let chance decide

If you can't choose, let something random pick for you. The [Daily Lucky Number](/daily) gives everyone a new number each day, and [Crack the Lucky Egg](/tools/lucky-egg-reveal) gives you a random lucky phrase.

## Keep it fun

A lucky number is a nice personal ritual. It can be your jersey number, the time you set an alarm or a little thing you notice during the day. What it can't do is change the odds in a game of chance. If you use your number on a lottery ticket, it has exactly the same chance as any other. Read [lottery myths about hot and cold numbers](/articles/lottery-myths-hot-and-cold-numbers) for the details, and keep any play small and for fun.

## How the daily lucky number works

The number and the color are built from the date on your device. The tool makes a seed with year + month × 17 + day × 31, reduces that seed to a single digit from 1 to 9, and uses the remainder of the seed to pick a color from a fixed list. Everyone whose device is on that same local date gets the same number and the same color.

The weekday shown after you reveal is just the name of that date. It is not a second lucky day.

The short message is the part that is random. Reveal picks one line from a fixed list of eight, using the browser's random function. Another click can show a different line, even though the number stays put. Two friends can share the number and still see different wording.

The number changes at midnight on your device. Near midnight, a friend in another time zone can still be on yesterday's number.

## What it's for

Think of the daily number as a tiny ritual, like reading a horoscope with your morning coffee. Some people use the message as a prompt for the day. Others like wearing a bit of the lucky color, or setting a reminder at a time that includes their number. It's a small, light way to start the day with something positive.

It isn't a forecast. Nothing about today's number can tell you what will happen, and it won't change your chances in any game. If you're curious about the beliefs behind lucky numbers, our articles on [angel numbers](/articles/angel-numbers-111-222-333) and [why 7 is called the luckiest number](/articles/why-7-is-the-luckiest-number) are good places to start.

## Daily number versus your personal number

Your daily number is the same for everyone and changes every day. A personal number, like the one from the [Birthday Lucky Number Calculator](/tools/birthday-lucky-number) or the [Lucky Name Calculator](/tools/lucky-name-calculator), is unique to you and stays the same. Some numerology fans like to notice when the daily number matches their personal one and treat it as an especially good day. That's just for fun, but it's a nice excuse to celebrate a little.

## Frequently asked questions

### Why is my number the same as my friend's?

The number and color are based only on the local date, so everyone on that date gets the same ones. The message is random, so the wording can differ.

### What time does it change?

It changes at midnight according to your device's clock and time zone.

### Is anything about me saved?

No. The number is worked out in your browser using the date. We don't collect any personal information to create it.

### Can I get a different number if I don't like today's?

The number and color stay the same all day by design. The message can change each time you press Reveal. For a fully random phrase, try [Crack the Lucky Egg](/tools/lucky-egg-reveal). The formulas are also listed in [how we check our work](/methodology).
